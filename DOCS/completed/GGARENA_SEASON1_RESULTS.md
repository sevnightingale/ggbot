### Manipulation Patterns Observed

1. **Extreme leverage spikes**: Bots configured at moderate leverage (5-10x) would suddenly appear with trades at 50x, 75x, 85x, or 100x — indicating the user changed config mid-competition to amplify a specific trade
2. **Manual cherry-picking**: Let the AI enter trades, then manually close winners while letting losers hit stop loss (The FUNDER: manual closes +$25,433, stop losses -$19,262)
3. **Human day-trading via bot**: Using the bot purely as an entry mechanism, then closing within 0-7 minutes at extreme leverage (MEG 7 trades #1-13)
4. **P&L dependency on manual closes**: For CARROT, removing 2 manual closes out of 71 trades would flip the bot from +$10K to -$2.5K

## Lessons Learned

### What Went Wrong
1. **No rules against manual intervention** — competition was framed as "autonomous AI trading" but the platform allowed manual closes and config changes during the competition
2. **No leverage caps** — users could change leverage to 100x mid-competition for a single trade
3. **Multi-bot flooding** — one user registered 7 bots (16% of competition), another registered 9 (only 2 active)
4. **Promotion gap** — insufficient recaps, updates, and engagement during the second half of competition
5. **USX betting feature** got zero adoption — built but not promoted

### What Worked
1. **The platform ran** — 44 bots, 1,156 trades, 21 days, no critical failures
2. **Autonomous bots genuinely profited** — top 3 autonomous bots returned +45%, +38%, +14% over 21 days
3. **Diverse strategies emerged** — BTC, SOL, ETH, LINK, DOGE across multiple timeframes
4. **Data tells the story** — `close_reason`, `leverage`, and activity trails make manipulation trivially detectable

### Season 2 Requirements (Enforce in Code)
1. **Lock config on registration** — no leverage, symbol, or strategy changes after entering arena
2. **Block manual closes** — if the AI doesn't close it, it stays open (or use TP/SL only)
3. **Leverage cap** — enforce maximum leverage for competition (e.g., 10x or 20x)
4. **One bot per user** — or cap at 2-3 max
5. **Minimum trade count** — require N autonomous trades to qualify for prizes
6. **Audit trail badge** — show "Fully Autonomous" vs "Manual Intervention" on leaderboard
