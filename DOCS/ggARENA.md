# ggArena - AI vs Human Trading Competition

## Concept Overview
Industry-wide AI vs Human trading competition featuring league-based system with progressive capital allocation. Positions as the defining moment when AI challenges human supremacy in trading (following Deep Blue/chess, AlphaGo/Go, OpenAI Five/esports).

**Strategic Evolution**: From standalone ggbots.ai feature → Ecosystem convening event with multi-platform partnerships.

## League Structure
### League 1 (Paper Trading)
- **Entry**: No fee practice mode OR entry fee with profit sharing
- **Participants**: Traders and Bot Builders
- **Capital**: Paper trading only
- **Advancement**: Qualified participants invited to League 2

### League 2+ (Live Trading)
- **Capital**: Starting with $100 real money per participant
- **Execution**: Proxy account system - participants make decisions, platform executes
- **Profit sharing**: Participants share profits, platform takes percentage
- **Relegation**: Unprofitable traders moved back to League 1

## Scaling Vision
- **Long-term**: Elite participants trading with $100k+ each
- **Mega seasons**: Tens of millions in total capital
- **Platform sustainability**: Revenue from percentage of aggregate profits

## Business Model Requirements
- **Profitability dependency**: Platform requires group profitability to sustain
- **Revenue source**: Percentage of trading profits
- **Capital progression**: Higher leagues enable larger capital allocation

## Target Audience Strategy

### Two Potential Markets
**Unskilled Traders (Learn-to-Earn Model)**
- Larger market, easier to attract
- Aligns with [[raza-open-campus]] original vision: "How to make money when you have no money"
- Viral video validation: Many comments asked "can I use this if I'm not good at trading?"
- 1000 true fans pathway: Build broad base of retail traders

**Skilled Traders (Competition Model)**
- Smaller but more immediately competitive
- "Can you beat the bots?" appeal
- Harder to attract without existing reputation

### Strategic Decision: Start Broad, Funnel Narrow
**Primary Target**: Unskilled traders (consumer-focused, retail, high volume)
- Build attention and audience with accessible entry point
- Use [[chess-com-trading-app-idea]] concept: Train against bots, learn by doing
- Potential app name: "Profit Dojo" or keep ggArena branding

**Secondary Funnel**: Skilled traders through targeted channels
- KOL partnerships to attract experienced traders
- Proven performers from unskilled tier graduate to competitive leagues
- Partnership with existing trading communities

## Ecosystem Convening Strategy

**Instead of**: Sev's ggArena competing for individual traders in isolation

**Execute**: Industry-wide AI Trading Competition event

### Partnership Categories

**Tier 2 - Bot Builder Platforms**

## Product Development Layers

### Layer 1: Competition Platform (ggArena Core)
League-based competitive trading - bots vs humans with progressive capital allocation

### Layer 2: Learning Platform (Potential "Profit Dojo")
Chess.com-inspired training app:
- Paper trade against AI bots of different skill levels
- Trading ELO scoring system
- Gamified progression mechanics
- Cyber-samurai/brutalist noir aesthetic

### Layer 3: Graduation Pipeline
Top performers from learning platform → Invited to ggArena leagues → Capital provision for proven traders

**Note**: Full app development takes time. Landing page + waitlist for Season 1 competition is faster initial execution.
