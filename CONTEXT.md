If I were to approach market analysis through astrology (often called financial astrology or astro-economics), I'd treat it as a timing and sentiment overlay rather than a standalone predictive system. Markets are driven by human psychology—fear, greed, herd behavior—and astrology offers a framework for mapping collective mood shifts via planetary cycles, aspects, and transits. It's correlative (patterns repeat historically), not causal, and works best combined with technical/fundamental analysis, risk management, and position sizing.Here's a step-by-step method I'd use, drawing from established financial astrology practices:1. Personal trader chart (if applying to your own decisions): Overlay transits on your natal chart to spot periods of clear judgment vs. clouded thinking (e.g., avoid big trades during Mercury retrograde hitting your Mercury).
Mundane/world charts: Reference ingress charts (planets entering new signs) or major cycles like Saturn-Neptune conjunctions for broader economic themes.

Mercury — Trading, communication, quick moves. Retrogrades link to miscommunications, reversals, or choppy/erratic action (avoid major entries; good for reviewing positions).
Venus — Value, money flows, luxury/consumer sectors. Harmonious aspects (trines/sextiles) support rallies in risk assets; hard aspects (squares/oppositions) can pressure sentiment.
Mars — Momentum, aggression, risk-on/risk-off. Conjunctions or hard aspects often spike volatility or trend changes.
Jupiter — Expansion, optimism, bull markets. Transits signal growth phases.
Saturn — Contraction, fear, bear markets/corrections. Hard aspects often mark tops or prolonged weakness.
Uranus — Sudden shocks, innovation (crypto/tech booms/crashes). In Taurus (until mid-2025) it shook traditional finance; now in Gemini, it may disrupt communication/currency mechanisms.
Neptune — Illusion, speculation, bubbles/dissolution. Ingress into Aries (late Jan 2026) historically ties to idealism turning chaotic.
Pluto — Transformation, power (big finance, crypto revolutions). In Aquarius (ongoing) emphasizes decentralized/tech-driven shifts.

Faster planets (Moon/Mercury/Venus/Mars) drive short-term trades; outer planets set multi-year themes.3. Track Major Aspects and CyclesHard aspects (conjunctions, squares, oppositions) → Volatility, reversals, or trend exhaustion.
Soft aspects (trines, sextiles) → Smooth trends, buying opportunities.
Eclipses → High-impact turning points (often reversals near solar/lunar eclipses).
Retrogrades → Delays, introspection (Mercury Rx for comms/tech glitches; Venus Rx for value reassessments).
Lunar phases → Daily sentiment: Full Moons heighten emotions/volatility.
Planetary ingresses → Sign changes reset energy (e.g., Saturn-Neptune at 0° Aries in Feb 2026 is a rare 35–6000-year event; often structural shifts).

Medium-term (weeks-months): Sun/Mars/Venus cycles + Mercury stations.
Long-term (quarters-years): Outer-planet aspects (e.g., Jupiter-Saturn squares for economic rotation).
Sector rotation via zodiac: Aries season → tech/innovation; Taurus → commodities; etc.

Risk first: Astrology highlights potential sentiment windows, not guarantees. Always use stops, diversify, and never bet the farm on a transit.
