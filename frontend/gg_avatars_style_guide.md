ggbots Visual Style Guide
Ceremonial Brutalism AutomataCore AestheticStyle Name: Guild Hall Portraits — Victorian scientific illustration meets Renaissance portraitThe Concept: Ancient brass automata built by a forgotten trading guild centuries ago. These are not sleek sci-fi robots. They are ceremonial machines — built with craftsmanship, worn by time, designed for specific purposes. Each one a specialist. Each one revered.Mood: Ancient. Prestigious. Silent. Powerful. Weathered. Purposeful.Color Palette (Strict)ColorHexUsageObsidian Black#0b0b0cBackground only. Pure black void. No gradients, no environments.Brass Gold#c1a87dPrimary metal. Body plates, instruments, details.Aged Brass#a89168Darker brass for shadows, recesses, patina.Light Brass#d4bc91Highlights, polished surfaces, accents.Ivory#edebe7Minimal use — engravings, subtle highlights, text on metal.Rule: No other colors. No blue glows. No red accents. No green patina. Brass, obsidian, ivory only.Material TexturesPrimary Material: Aged Brass

Matte metallic finish, not chrome or mirror-shine
Visible patina — subtle discoloration from centuries of existence
Fine scratches and wear marks on high-contact surfaces
Brushed metal texture on flat plates
Secondary Material: Obsidian Plating

Deep black metal plates interspersed with brass
Smooth, slightly reflective but not glossy
Used for contrast — alternating with brass sections
Surface Details:

Visible rivets and seams where plates join
Engraved patterns (charts, symbols, text too small to read)
Exposed gears and clockwork in chest cavities or joints
Hydraulic tubes and cables at joints (brass-colored)
Wear Patterns:

Heavier patina in recesses
Polished spots where frequently touched
Dust accumulation in crevices (subtle)
No rust — brass doesn't rust
Anatomical GuidelinesProportions:

Humanoid but not human
Slightly elongated limbs acceptable
Broader shoulders than humans typical
No organic curves — angular, mechanical joints
Head Designs:

Never a human face
Use: visors, lens arrays, speaker grilles, smooth plates, geometric shapes
No eyes — use alternatives: single slits, lens apparatus, compound sensors, blank plates
Can include: horns, antennae, dials, dishes, measurement instruments
Torso:

Often partially exposed — visible clockwork, gears, chambers
Central chest feature common (vault door, compass rose, engraved plate, resonating chamber)
Shoulders can have attachments (dishes, orreries, armor plates)
Arms & Hands:

Hands can be: normal mechanical hands, replaced with instruments/tools, gauntlets
Joints clearly mechanical — visible pistons, cables, hinges
Forearms can house instruments or have engraved panels
Legs:

Grounded, stable stances
Can be: standard humanoid, backward-jointed (bird-like), heavily armored
Wide stances suggest stability
Narrow stances suggest alertness
Pose GuidelinesDefault: Static and Iconic

Frontal or 3/4 view
Symmetrical or near-symmetrical composition
Centered in frame
Not action poses — these are portraits, not scenes
Acceptable Poses:

Standing neutral (most common)
Arms at sides
Arms crossed (defiance — The Contrarian)
Hands raised, palms up (receiving — The Herald, The Arbiter)
One arm raised pointing (navigation — The Compass)
Protective stance, hands forward (guarding — The Sentinel)
Slight lean forward (observation — The Technician)
Mid-stride, weight shifting (movement — The Nomad only)
Avoid:

Dynamic action poses
Running, jumping, fighting
Extreme angles
Crouching
Composition RulesBackground: Pure black void (#0b0b0c). No exceptions. No environments, no floors, no gradients, no atmospheric effects.Framing:

Full body or 3/4 body (cut at mid-thigh)
Centered subject
Adequate negative space around figure
Figure should occupy 60-75% of vertical frame
Lighting:

Single dramatic light source (implied, not visible)
Coming from upper left or upper right
Creates defined shadows on opposite side
Highlights brass surfaces facing light
Deep shadows in recesses and opposite side
No:

Multiple light sources
Rim lighting
Dramatic backlighting
Lens flares
God rays
Character DifferentiationEach bot should be instantly recognizable by silhouette. Differentiate through:1. Head Design

Unique head shape/apparatus for each bot
The Technician: Magnifying lens array
The Herald: Tall antenna horns + speaker grille mouth
The Sentinel: Reinforced dome with visor slit
The Contrarian: Smooth mask with diagonal slit, tilted head
The Arbiter: Split face (brass/obsidian), scale bars from temples
The Compass: Telescopic eye + astrolabe dial eye
The Nomad: Hooded cowl, face in shadow
2. Torso Feature

Central chest element unique to each
The Technician: Exposed clockwork + price chart engraving
The Herald: Pipe organ resonating chambers
The Sentinel: Vault door
The Contrarian: Opposing arrows pattern
The Arbiter: Legal document engravings + judicial robes
The Compass: Compass rose + star charts
The Nomad: Patchwork plates + hanging artifacts
3. Hand Design

The Technician: Precision instruments (calipers, probes)
The Herald: Thin sensor-tipped fingers, open palms
The Sentinel: Heavy gauntlets, protective stance
The Contrarian: One fist clenched, one open
The Arbiter: Holding scale dishes
The Compass: Sextant fused to palm
The Nomad: Mismatched arms (different eras)
4. Stance/Posture

The Technician: Leaning slightly forward, observational
The Herald: Upright, alert, receiving
The Sentinel: Wide stance, rooted, unmovable
The Contrarian: Asymmetrical, defiant, weight shifted
The Arbiter: Perfectly centered, symmetrical, still
The Compass: Steady, one arm pointing to horizon
The Nomad: Mid-stride, never fully still
5. Wear Level

Most bots: Moderate patina, centuries of careful use
The Nomad: Heavy wear, repairs, mismatched parts, tattered edges
The Sentinel: Some battle marks, scratches from protection
The Arbiter: Most pristine, gravitas of age but well-maintained
Style ReferencesArt Historical:

Victorian scientific illustration (detailed, technical, precise)
Renaissance portraiture (dignified poses, dark backgrounds)
Alchemical engravings (mysterious symbols, arcane instruments)
Fictional:

Robots from Laputa: Castle in the Sky (ancient, powerful, patient)
Maschinenmensch from Metropolis (ceremonial, iconic)
Dark Souls armor aesthetics (weathered, imposing, non-human)
Brass automata from Victorian exhibitions
What We Are NOT:

Star Wars droids (too friendly, too sci-fi)
Terminator (too aggressive, too chrome)
Iron Man suits (too sleek, too modern)
Steampunk aesthetic (too busy, too many gears everywhere)
Anime mecha (too dynamic, too colorful)

Creating New Bot VisualsWhen designing a new ggbot, answer these questions first:
What is this bot's core philosophy? (One sentence)
What instrument or object symbolizes this philosophy? (For hands/chest)
What head design reflects how this bot "sees" the market?
What posture reflects this bot's relationship to trading? (Aggressive, defensive, patient, alert, etc.)
How worn is this bot? (Pristine, moderate, heavy)
What single word describes the silhouette? (Fortress, hunter, judge, wanderer, etc.)
